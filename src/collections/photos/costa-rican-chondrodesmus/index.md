Chondrodesmus is a genius of the order Polydesmida that can be seen only in Central America.

Members of the order Polydesmida are also known as flat-backed millipedes, because on most species, each body segment has wide lateral keels known as paranota.

The order Polydesmida belongs to the Diplopoda class of arthropods, also called Millipedes (originating from the Latin mille, "thousand", and pes, "foot"). They are characterised by having two pairs of jointed legs on most body segments.

This specimen was around 5 centimeters long, pretty impressive first animal encounter in Costa Rica.
